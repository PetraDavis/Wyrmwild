# Wyrmwild

A 3D dragon-taming game that runs in the browser. Explore lands, find eggs,
hatch and raise dragons, ride them and fly.

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (or turn on GitHub Pages for this
repository and open the Pages link).

## Controls

- **W A S D** or arrow keys: move
- **Shift**: run (on a dragon in the air: fly down)
- **Click the game** to look with the mouse (Esc frees the mouse), **scroll**: zoom
- **E**: pick up / use
- **R**: ride your dragon or get off (on the ground)
- **Space**: take off and fly up
- **Shift twice quickly** while flying high: dive bomb (let go of Shift to pull up)

## What's in it so far

Green Valley: find the glowing egg, carry it to your nest, hatch and name your
dragon, walk with it until it grows up, then ride and fly it.

Dragon kinds: the egg can hatch any of 28 normal kinds (Common to Legendary;
rarer kinds hatch less often). Each kind has its own body shape, colours, size,
speed, HP, claw and breath damage, and its own growth per level. Rarer kinds
look fancier: glowing horns, wing bones, wing-edge glow and floating sparkles.

Sky Islands: one huge floating island (Sky Haven) and five small ones high
above Green Valley, with pink
blossom trees, glowing crystals, waterfalls spilling into the sky and an old
stone ring on Sky Haven, the biggest one. Fly up on your dragon to reach them,
land and walk around. Walk off the edge and you fall back down to the valley.

`monsters.html` is a viewer for the 17 monster models (not in the game yet),
each with the adaptations it has for the land it lives in.

The Village: west of the nest along the dirt road. Twelve buildings (quest
hall, shop, bakery, smithy, mayor's house, mapmaker's tower, farm, cottages
and Old Nettle's crooked hut) and thirteen villagers who walk around and talk.
Press E near someone to talk; nobody repeats a line until you've heard them all.

Saving: progress saves in your browser every few seconds. Press Continue on the
start screen to carry on, or New game to start over.

Dragon speed: every kind of dragon has a speed from 1 to 5 (sky and water
dragons are fastest, heavy ones slowest).

The Ocean: a gap in the mountains south-east of the nest opens onto a sandy
beach with palm trees, waves, sea stacks, jumping fish and a lighthouse out in
the bay. You can fly far out over the water.
